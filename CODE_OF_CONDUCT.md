# Code of Conduct

We want Lifescape to be a welcoming project. Everyone taking part agrees to:

- be respectful and assume good faith;
- give and accept feedback on the work, not the person;
- not harass, demean, or discriminate against anyone;
- not publish another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, and contributions that break these rules, and may
block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting on
`buildproven/lifescape` or contact the maintainers through their GitHub profile.

This text is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
